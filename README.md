# Market Incentives Survey

**Goal**  
Produce a 3 to 4 page brief that compares incentive options and justifies our chosen design for this quarter.

**Definition of done**
- Brief fits in 3 to 4 pages with a one-page executive summary
- Comparison matrix completed with 3 to 5 options
- Stakeholder map lists at least one public buyer, one funder, one private buyer
- Clear recommendation with decision criteria

## Comparison matrix template

| Option | Example in the wild | Buyer type | Enforceability | Cost to run | Time to pilot | Fit for AI safety | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Procurement scoring bonus | City climate tenders | Public | High | Low | Short | Strong | Uses rubric |
| AMC with gates | Vaccine AMCs | Funder | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | Needs milestones |
| Prize | X-prize style | Funder | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | Clear win rule |
| Preferred supplier tier | Gov supplier lists | Public | High | Low | Short | Strong | Needs attestation |
| Insurance-like pool | Parametric models | Private | Low | High | Long | Experimental | Pricing risk is hard |

**Decision criteria**  
- Actionability in 12 weeks  
- Verification burden  
- Political and legal feasibility  
- External interest and path to impact
